# Audio licences

Every file in this folder is **CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication)**.
That permits use in commercial projects, including paid distribution on Steam,
with no attribution required and no licence file to ship.

Attribution is given here anyway because it costs nothing and the packs are
worth pointing at.

## Adding more

Keep to CC0. [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/category:Audio) publishes ten
audio packs, all CC0, and [Freesound](https://freesound.org) has a CC0 filter
in its licence facet. Anything under CC-BY would mean shipping an attribution
screen, and anything under a non-commercial licence cannot go on a storefront
at all — record the licence in this file when you add a file.
